# Glossary

## Agent Authority Infrastructure

The runtime authority, approval, and tamper-evident evidence layer for AI agents.

## Authority

The definition of what an AI agent is allowed to do before execution.

## Approval

The runtime decision that resolves an intended action as allow, deny, or human review.

## Evidence

The signed, tamper-evident record that shows what was attempted, what decision was made, and what happened afterward.

## Action Receipt

An evidence record associated with a specific attempted agent action. This repository discusses action receipts conceptually and does not publish a canonical product schema.

## Evidence Pack

A bundle of records, policy context, decisions, denials, approvals, and verification status prepared for security review, audit, or pilot closeout.

## Human Review

A runtime path where an intended action is held until a named reviewer approves or rejects it.

## Mission Scope

The defined purpose, boundary, and operating context for an AI agent.

## Tamper-Evident

Structured so that alteration or deletion can be detected. Prefer this term over "immutable."

## Verifiable Evidence

Evidence that can be inspected and checked by a reviewer. Prefer this term over "verifiable proof" in body copy.
